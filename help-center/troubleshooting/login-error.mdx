---
title: Login Error - SPID/CIE Not Working
description: How to resolve failed login attempts.
---

## Symptoms

- Error message after login attempt
- "Autenticazione fallita" shown on screen

## Causes

- Incorrect credentials
- SPID/CIE service downtime
- Browser cookies disabled

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check credentials">
    Ensure correct username/password for your SPID or CIE.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try a different browser">
    Some browsers may block login popups. Use Chrome or Firefox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear cookies">
    Enable cookies and clear browser cache, then retry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check SPID/CIE service status">
    Visit your provider's status page for updates.
  </Step>
</Steps>
