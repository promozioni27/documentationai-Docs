---
title: How do I access my Fascicolo Sanitario?
description: Quick guide to accessing your digital health record.
---

## Access Steps

1. Go to `https://ilmiofascicolosanitario.com`
2. Click "Accedi al Fascicolo"
3. Log in using SPID or CIE credentials

## Requirements

- Valid SPID or CIE account

## Note

You must have activated your Fascicolo Sanitario with your local health authority for first access.
