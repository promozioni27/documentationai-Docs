## Add a Document

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Access your Fascicolo Sanitario via `https://ilmiofascicolosanitario.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to 'Documenti' section">
    In the main sidebar, click on "Documenti".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload file">
    Click "Aggiungi documento" and select your PDF or image.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete details">
    Fill in required fields (type, date) and confirm upload.
  </Step>
</Steps>
